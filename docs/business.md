# Munyewu Travel & Tours

![Munyewu Travel & Tours logo](munyewu-logo.png)

Munyewu Travel & Tours is a Cape Town travel and private transport company based in Thornton. Travellers can book a city or Winelands tour, arrange an airport transfer, or enquire about private chauffeur services.

## Services and vehicles

| Customer need | Advertised services |
| --- | --- |
| Explore Cape Town and the Winelands | City tours and Winelands tours |
| Get to or from the airport | Airport transfers |
| Arrange a private journey | Private transport, luxury travel and private chauffeur services |
| Ask about a particular trip | Listed on the banner as “Specific Travel”. Confirm the itinerary when enquiring. |

The banner features a luxury car, an SUV and an 11-seater. Confirm the vehicle and passenger count when booking.

## Brand colours

Use these proportions across the designed surface of a page, brochure or social post. The hex values are suggested digital matches to the photographed banner.

| Share | Colour | Hex | Application |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 70% | Midnight navy | `#24283D` | Main background, including the area around the logo |
| 20% | Soft white | `#F5F4F0` | Headings and body text on navy, plus light panels for trip details |
| 10% | Golden yellow | `#E3B743` | Booking buttons, selected words and small accents |

For a tour promotion, set the destination name in white on navy and place a gold **Enquire about a tour** button beneath it with navy lettering. Use the supplied logo crop at its original proportions. It comes from the printed banner and is best suited to small placements.
