## The one goal

Turn a visitor into a qualified enquiry, and a qualified enquiry into a booked client. Everything on the site serves four outcomes, in order: **be found, be trusted, make it effortless to enquire, convert the enquiry**. A change that does not help one of these does not ship.

The primary conversion is a completed enquiry by any path: form submit, WhatsApp open or call tap. Treat all three as wins and measure them separately.

## Brand

- **Colours** — navy `#24283D` (70%), paper `#F5F4F0` (20%), gold `#E3B743` (10%). Gold is reserved for the action; never spend it on decoration.
- **Type** — Bebas Neue for display (hero and section titles; uppercase by nature) paired with Inter for all body, card titles, labels and UI. System sans-serif fallback. Inter text is sentence case; never set running text in Bebas Neue.
- **Logo** — supplied banner crop; place on navy, preserve aspect ratio, keep ≥12px clear space.
- **Voice** — calm, premium, specific and trustworthy. Real destinations and vehicles, never stock clichés or lorem ipsum.

## Standing decisions

- Enquiry-led, not e-commerce. There is no online checkout or payment; the conversion is a qualified enquiry.
- WhatsApp and click-to-call are first-class conversion paths, not afterthoughts, because this market prefers them.
- Every CTA carries the chosen service into the enquiry so the operator can reply with a specific price.
- Only publish claims and reviews the operator can stand behind. No invented testimonials, ratings or credentials — ever. This protects both conversion and search trust.
- Motion is purposeful and always degrades under `prefers-reduced-motion`; the hero never autoplays.
- Mobile mid-range Android on a slow connection is the reference device for every decision.
